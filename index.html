隐私政策

应用名称：厨邦帮（CookBang） · 最近更新：2026-10-26 · 发布方：InfoMacro

本隐私政策说明您在使用厨邦帮 Android 应用（下称「本应用」）时，我们如何处理相关信息。使用本应用即表示您理解并同意本政策。

1. 概述

厨邦帮提供：

  • 菜谱浏览与搜索（通过公开 HowToCook API）
  • 保存在本机的烹饪笔记
  • 应用内打包的烹饪技巧内容
  • 可选的「AI 厨师」功能：可将文字及（经您主动选择的）菜品照片发送至第三方 AI 服务，获取烹饪相关回答

本应用不要求注册账号，也不会出售您的个人信息。

2. 我们处理的信息

2.1 仅保存在您设备上的信息

烹饪笔记（标题、文字、您添加的图片）
用途：供您记录烹饪心得
是否共享：否（除非您自行导出或分享）

菜谱列表缓存
用途：提升浏览体验、弱网可用
是否共享：否

语言偏好
用途：记住界面语言
是否共享：否

2.2 使用网络功能时处理的信息

菜谱与搜索相关请求
何时：浏览或搜索菜谱时
用途：获取菜谱内容
接收方：HowToCook 公开 API（api.howtocook.cn）及图片 CDN

AI 厨师中您输入的文字
何时：发送消息时
用途：生成烹饪建议
接收方：SiliconFlow AI API（api.siliconflow.cn）

AI 厨师中您附加的照片（拍照或选图）
何时：仅当您主动添加图片时
用途：识菜、结合图像回答烹饪问题
接收方：SiliconFlow AI API

基础技术数据（如 IP、TLS 连接元数据）
何时：连接服务器时自动产生
用途：完成并保障传输安全
接收方：网络运营商及上述 API 运营方

我们无意收集：姓名、邮箱、电话、精确位置、通讯录、短信、通话记录，或用于广告画像的广告 ID。

2.3 权限说明

网络 / 网络状态
用于加载菜谱、调用 AI 服务。

相机
可选。用于为 AI 厨师拍摄菜品照片。

相册选图使用系统文档或照片选择器（SAF），不需要申请读取全部照片或存储读权限。

您可拒绝相机权限；菜谱浏览、笔记与技巧仍可使用。未授权时仅无法使用 AI 拍照；从相册选图无需存储权限。

3. AI 处理说明

若您使用 AI 厨师：

  • 您提交的内容（文字及可选图片）将传输至 SiliconFlow 以生成回复。
  • 请勿提交敏感个人信息、未经授权的他人隐私照片、医疗诊断诉求或违法内容。
  • AI 回答仅供一般烹饪参考，不构成专业营养、医疗或食品安全认证意见。
  • 图片用于当次请求处理；本应用当前版本不运营独立的云端相册来保存您的照片。

4. 儿童隐私

本应用面向对烹饪感兴趣的一般用户，并非专门面向 13 岁以下儿童（或您所在地区的同等年龄门槛）。我们不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。如您认为儿童通过本应用提供了个人信息，请联系我们以便协助处理本机数据清理指引。

5. 保存期限

  • 本机笔记与缓存在您删除内容或卸载应用前会保留。
  • 网络请求由第三方 API 按其自身政策处理。当前版本不在厨邦帮自有云端长期保存您的聊天或笔记。

6. 安全

API 通信使用 HTTPS。任何传输或存储方式都无法保证百分之百安全，请妥善保管并及时更新设备。

This Privacy Policy explains how CookBang (“we”, “the App”) handles information when you use the Android application. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

1. Overview

CookBang is a cooking helper that provides:

  • Recipe browsing and search (via the public HowToCook API)
  • Local cooking notes stored on your device
  • Cooking tips packaged in the App
  • An optional “AI Chef” feature that can send text and, if you choose, a food photo to a third-party AI service for cooking-related answers

We do not require you to create an account. We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information we process

2.1 Information stored only on your device

Cooking notes (title, text, images you add)
Purpose: Let you keep personal cooking notes
Shared with others: No (unless you export or share yourself)

Recipe list cache
Purpose: Faster browsing and weak-network use
Shared with others: No

Language preference
Purpose: Remember your UI language
Shared with others: No

2.2 Information processed when you use network features

Recipe and search requests
When: While you browse or search recipes
Purpose: Fetch recipe content
Recipients: HowToCook public API (api.howtocook.cn) and image CDN

Text you type in AI Chef
When: When you send a message
Purpose: Generate cooking guidance
Recipients: SiliconFlow AI API (api.siliconflow.cn)

Photos you attach in AI Chef (camera or gallery)
When: Only when you explicitly add an image
Purpose: Dish recognition and image-based cooking answers
Recipients: SiliconFlow AI API

Basic technical data (e.g. IP address, TLS metadata)
When: Automatically when connecting to servers
Purpose: Deliver and secure the request
Recipients: Network providers and the API operators above

We do not intentionally collect: name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, SMS, call logs, or advertising IDs for ads profiling.

2.3 Permissions

Internet / network state
Used to load recipes and call AI services.

Camera
Optional. Used to take a dish photo for AI Chef.

Gallery selection uses the system document or photo picker (SAF) and does not require broad photo library or storage read permission.

You can deny camera permission; recipe browsing, notes, and tips still work. Without camera access, AI capture is unavailable; gallery pick does not need storage permission.

3. AI processing notice

If you use AI Chef:

  • Content you submit (text and optional image) is transmitted to SiliconFlow to generate a response.
  • Do not submit sensitive personal data, third-party private photos without permission, medical advice requests, or illegal content.
  • AI answers are for general cooking reference only and are not professional dietary, medical, or food-safety certification.
  • Images are processed for the request; we do not operate a separate cloud album of your photos inside CookBang.

4. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience interested in cooking. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child provided personal data through the App, contact us so we can help with on-device data removal guidance.

5. Data retention

  • On-device notes and cache remain until you delete them or uninstall the App.
  • Network requests are processed by third-party APIs under their own retention practices. We do not operate a CookBang user cloud database for your chats or notes in the current version.

6. Security

We use HTTPS for API communication. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please keep your device locked and updated.

7. Third-party services

Role: Recipes and images
Policy: Operator of howtocook.cn services

Role: AI chat and vision
Policy: https://siliconflow.cn (see their privacy terms)

Role: Distribution, system photo picker, and camera

8. Your choices

  • Change language in Settings → Language
  • Clear notes by deleting them in the Notes tab
  • Revoke camera permission in system App settings
  • Uninstall the App to remove local databases and cache
